# PeopleTrack for payroll and finance

## Set-up (payroll officer)
**Payroll → Payroll setup**: pay components (earnings, deductions, benefits, employer
contributions; fixed, percentage or formula), statutory rate tables (PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, Housing
Levy, NITA, with effective dates), payment file formats and the account mapping for the
journal. **Pay and loans** sets each person's salary, recurring items, loans and one-off items.

## The monthly payroll
1. **Payroll runs → New payroll** for the month, then **Calculate**. Leave without pay, overtime
   from attendance (where rules allow), loans, one-off items, expense reimbursements and
   medical contributions are included. Check the warnings and the **variance** against last
   month; recalculate as often as needed.
2. **Submit for approval**: the HR manager checks it, then a finance approver approves it.
   The submitter cannot approve. Staff are told their payslips are ready.
3. Download the **bank file** (Excel), **M-Pesa** file, and the statutory returns (**P10**,
   **NSSF**, **SHA**, **Housing Levy**), the **payroll register** and the **journal**.
4. After paying, **Mark paid**, then **Close**.

## Finance
* Approve payroll and the finance step of trips with an advance and expense claims under
  **Approvals**; receipts open from the claim.
* **Expense claims** lists claims and trips in your area. Approved claims are paid as a
  non-taxable item in the next open payroll month.
* The journal per run is available as a file and through the API for the finance system.
* **Reports → Payroll cost** gives cost by department and cost centre per month.
