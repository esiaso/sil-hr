# PeopleTrack for line managers and heads of department

Everything in the [staff guide](staff.md), plus:

## Approvals
**Approvals** lists requests waiting for you: leave, attendance corrections, training places,
trips and expense claims, appraisals, promotions and transfers, requisitions. Open one to see
the details and **Approve**, **Return for correction** or **Reject** (a comment is required for
the last two). Requests sent to a team (for example "HR manager") can be taken by anyone in it.
If nobody acts within the escalation time, the request moves up to the next manager.
**Delegations** (same page) hands your approvals to a colleague while you are away.

## Your team
* **Leave → Leave requests** and the team calendar show your team's leave.
* **Time & attendance → Daily attendance** shows who was present, late or absent; **Rosters**
  plans shifts for your department.
* **Staff directory** finds anyone in the hospital.

## Appraisals you write
**My performance → Appraisals I write**: agree each person's goals (**Agree goals**; weights
must total 100), then after their self-review score each goal and write your assessment.
**Submit for approval** sends it to your own manager and HR, who may moderate the rating. If the
self-review is late, you can assess without it after its due date.

## Requests you can raise
* **Recruitment → Requisitions**: ask to fill a post (department head, HR, finance approve).
* **Lifecycle → Promotions and changes**: request a promotion, transfer, confirmation,
  contract renewal, resignation or termination.

## Reports (heads of department)
**Reports** gives headcount, turnover, leave liability, licence and contract expiry for your
department, as tables or CSV/Excel downloads.
