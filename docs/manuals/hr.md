# PeopleTrack for HR

## People
* **People → Employees → Hire** adds an employee (a staff number is given automatically).
  Open a record to set the manager, contacts, documents, bank details, contracts and pay.
* **Licences** tracks practising licences; reminders go out before expiry, and the HMIS sees
  the status. **Contact changes** approves changes staff make to their own details.
* **Import & export** loads or downloads employees in bulk (CSV or Excel).

## Leave, attendance
* **Leave → Leave setup**: leave types, entitlements by grade and type, public holidays.
  Adjust a balance from the employee's leave page.
* **Time & attendance**: shifts per department, rosters, overtime rules; device connectors
  are under **Integrations**.

## Lifecycle
**Contracts** (renewal reminders 60 days ahead), **Promotions and changes**, **Onboarding and
offboarding** checklists (IT, facilities, finance and the line manager each get their tasks),
**Exits** (clearance including assets held in procurement, final dues, completion, which ends
access), **Letters** from templates (PDF or Word), and **My HR file** cases (warnings,
improvement plans, awards, complaints with restricted access).

## Recruitment
See the [recruiters' guide](recruiters.md).

## Performance and training
* **Review cycles**: create a cycle (all staff or one department), **Launch** it to create the
  reviews, **Open self-reviews**, then follow progress and the rating distribution. During
  approval, set a moderated final rating with a reason (**Calibration**). **Indicator library**
  holds the KPIs staff can pick as goals.
* **Training sessions**: programmes (CPD points, pass marks, mandatory for all or clinical
  staff, how often), sessions, booking staff, recording attendance and scores, closing a
  session. **Mandatory training** shows who is overdue. **CPD** verifies outside claims and
  shows who is short of points; set each council's yearly requirement there.

## Workplace
**Announcements** (to everyone or chosen branches and departments, scheduled, pinned, with an
expiry), **Policies** (publish a PDF to all staff or departments; see who has not read it; a
new version asks everyone again), **Meeting rooms**.

## Reports
**Reports**: headcount (by department, branch, grade, type or gender, at any date), turnover,
leave liability, overtime cost, payroll cost, licence and contract expiry. Every report
downloads as CSV or Excel; downloads are recorded in the audit trail.

## Medical scheme
**Medical scheme → Members and applications** handles joining, dependants, top-ups and
over-limit approvals; **Scheme setup** defines schemes, categories, limits and contributions.
