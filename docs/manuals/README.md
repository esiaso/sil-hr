# PeopleTrack user manuals

Short guides for each kind of user. Everyone starts with **[Staff](staff.md)**; the other
guides add what a role can do on top. Menu names are those in the left-hand sidebar; you see
only the entries your role allows.

| Guide | For |
|---|---|
| [Staff](staff.md) | Everyone: profile, leave, attendance, payslips, training, expenses, policies, meetings |
| [Line managers and heads of department](managers.md) | Approving, your team's leave and attendance, appraisals, rosters |
| [HR](hr.md) | Employees, licences, contracts, lifecycle, recruitment, performance, training, policies, reports |
| [Payroll and finance](payroll.md) | Pay set-up, monthly payroll, payment and statutory files, journals, expenses |
| [Recruiters](recruiters.md) | Requisitions, adverts, candidates, interviews, offers |
| [Medical scheme billing](medical-billing.md) | Looking up cover and recording claims |
| [Administrators](administrators.md) | Users, roles, approval workflows, settings, integrations |

Sign in at your hospital's PeopleTrack address with your username and password (or the
single sign-on button if your hospital uses it). The first time, you are asked to choose a
new password. Forgotten passwords are reset by an administrator.
