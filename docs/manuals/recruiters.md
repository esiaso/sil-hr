# PeopleTrack for recruiters

1. **Requisitions**: managers ask to fill a post; once approved it can be advertised.
2. **Jobs and candidates → New advert**: write the advert, screening questions and closing
   date, and publish it. Published adverts appear on the public careers site
   (`/careers`), where candidates apply with a CV and give consent for their data.
3. Move applications through the pipeline (screening, shortlist, interview, assessment,
   offer). **Schedule interview** invites a panel; panel members give scores and a
   recommendation under **My interviews**. **Record assessment** stores test results.
4. **Make an offer**: it goes for approval (finance too when above the approved range) and
   produces the offer letter. When the candidate accepts, record it: they become an employee
   with a contract, salary and onboarding checklist from the start date.
5. Unsuccessful candidates' details are removed automatically after the retention period
   they agreed to (24 months by default).
