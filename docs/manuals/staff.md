# PeopleTrack for staff

## Your home page
**Dashboard** shows announcements, anything waiting for you (approvals, policies to read,
meeting actions) and your leave balance. **Announcements** lists everything published to you.

## Your profile
**My profile** shows your record, contacts, next of kin, qualifications and documents. To
change your phone, address or next of kin, edit them there: HR approves the change before it
takes effect. Your national ID and KRA PIN are shown masked.

## Leave
1. **My leave** → **Apply for leave**. Choose the type and the dates; the number of working
   days (public holidays and your rest days excluded) and your balance after are shown.
2. **Send for approval.** Your line manager (and anyone else in your hospital's chain) is
   told. You can cancel a request that has not started.
3. The team calendar under **Leave → Team calendar** shows who else is away.

## Clocking in and out
**Clock in / my attendance**: press **Clock in** when you arrive and **Clock out** when you
leave (your phone may ask to share your location). Punches from the clocking machines appear
here too. If you forgot to clock, use **Correct a day**; your manager approves the correction.

## Payslips
**My payslips** lists each month once payroll is approved. Open one to view or download it,
and download your P9 for the year from the same page.

## Training and CPD
**My training and CPD** shows your mandatory training (current, due soon, overdue), your
sessions, and sessions you can ask to join (**Ask for a place**; your line manager approves).
After a session, **Rate it**. Your CPD points for the year are shown against your council's
requirement; claim points for outside activities with **Claim CPD points** and a certificate.
You are reminded 60 days before your licence expires if you are short.

## Appraisals
**My performance** lists your reviews. In goal setting, add goals with your reviewer (their
weights must total 100) and keep progress up to date. When the self-review opens, score each
goal 1 to 5 and comment, then **Submit to my reviewer**. When the approved review comes back,
read it and **Acknowledge** it, saying if you disagree.

## Travel and expenses
**Travel and expenses** → **Plan a trip** before travelling (ask for an advance if needed).
After the trip, **Claim expenses**: add each item with its receipt (receipts are required over
KES 500), then **Submit for approval**. Approved claims are paid with your next salary, less
any advance; an unspent advance is recovered the same way.

## Policies
**Policies** lists the hospital policies that apply to you. Open each one and press **I have
read it**. A new version asks you again.

## Meetings and calendar
**Meetings** lists meetings you organise or are invited to: accept or decline, read the
agenda and minutes, and tick off actions assigned to you. **New meeting** books a room (a room
cannot be double-booked) and invites people. **My calendar** shows your leave, shifts,
meetings, training and interviews; **Subscribe in Outlook or Google** gives a private address
for your phone's calendar (keep it to yourself).

## Your HR file and medical cover
**My HR file** shows warnings, improvement plans and awards recorded for you, and lets you
raise a complaint (only the people HR names can see it). **My medical cover** shows your
scheme, dependants and how much of each limit is left.
